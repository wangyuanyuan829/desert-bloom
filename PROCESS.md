## 1. Starting idea

I wanted to adapt the pipe-connection puzzle mechanic into a small experience with an environmental theme. Instead of powering a lamp, the player guides water through pipe tiles to help a dry desert tree recover.

## 2. Design intention

- **Intended user:** someone who enjoys a short, casual puzzle.
- **User action:** click a pipe tile to rotate it.
- **Program response:** update the tile orientation and recompute the connected path.
- **Feedback:** mark tiles reached by water and show a success message when the water reaches the tree.
- **Reset:** let the player restart without refreshing the page.
